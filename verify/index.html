Verify a student credential

For employers, universities, banks and regulators. Scan the QR code on a certificate or enter its credential ID. You will see only the details the student has chosen to share, and the student is told that you checked.

Verification works offline too: the QR carries a signed credential that can be checked against the AICTE public key.
