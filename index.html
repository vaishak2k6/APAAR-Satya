AICTE · Academic year 2026–27

Student data management and verification for AICTE-approved institutions

Institutions submit admitted-student records after counselling. AICTE monitors intake, quotas and verification status in real time, and authorised verifiers confirm records with the student's consent.

Student-level records
Every admitted student is reported against an APAAR ID, not just institution totals.
Independent verification
Each record is checked against counselling, DigiLocker, scholarship and ABC data before it counts.
Seat-capped by design
Uploads beyond the seats approved in the Extension of Approval are rejected at submission.
Consent and audit
Students see who accessed their record. Every change is written to a tamper-evident log.
Submission window: First-year records for 2026–27 close on 15 November 2026. Lateral-entry records close on 30 November 2026.
